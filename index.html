About SR Tool

Savage Recovery Tool is a recovery-focused utility. It connects with authorized recovery services to check account status. It fetches available recovery information from the server. The tool verifies OTP and recovery-related restrictions. It helps diagnose temporary verification or delivery issues. No passwords, OTPs, cookies, or private credentials are stored. Designed for secure and authorized account recovery purposes.
